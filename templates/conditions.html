📜 Booking, Payment & Cancellation Policies

Transparent rules and mutual assurance standards protecting both customers and certified professionals.

📌 Core Platform Principles

To ensure complete trust and punctuality, our platform operates under four key rules:

💳

1. 20% Advance Token

When scheduling an appointment, a 20% Advance Token is paid securely online to reserve the professional's calendar and guarantee arrival.

⚡

2. Immediate Balance Settlement

Upon satisfactory completion of the service, the remaining 80% Balance Amount is settled immediately via UPI, QR scanner, or cash.

❌

3. Client Cancellation: Non-Refundable

If a client cancels a confirmed appointment, the advance token is Non-Refundable to compensate for the artisan's reserved time and transit prep.

✅

4. Professional Cancellation: 100% Refund

If an artisan or studio cancels an appointment for any reason, the customer receives an automatic 100% Full Refund back to their original account.
